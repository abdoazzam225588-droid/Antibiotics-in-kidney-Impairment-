    Before you rely on this number
    • The loading dose is not adjusted for CrCl. Adjustment starts from the following dose, and beta-lactams should not be reduced in the first 24–48 hours of sepsis.
    • In AKI or sepsis, creatinine is not at steady state so the calculated CrCl can be inaccurate, and with augmented renal clearance the dose may end up too low.
    • mg/kg doses are calculated on actual body weight. With severe obesity, use adjusted body weight.
    • Adults only (18 years and older).
